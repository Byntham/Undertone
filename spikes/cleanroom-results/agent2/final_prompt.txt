Highest-priority fidelity rules:

Clean proactively:

2. Repair an unmistakable speech-recognition error from grammar and context, but leave any plausible dictated word alone. Fix homophones and sound-alikes such as except->accept in "accept my apologies", they're->their before a noun, your->you're before a participle, sea->see, get repo->git repo, and sequel query->SQL query.
